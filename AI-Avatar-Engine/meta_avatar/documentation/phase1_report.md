## Toon-specific issues found & fixed (the real work)

1. **`Toon_Eyebrows` is a separate floating mesh** (realistic brows are
   texture). New `add_brow_follow_morphs.py` KNN-samples the body skin's
   displacement under every identity key and bakes matching follower keys
   (10 male / 11 female) — brows track `eyebrow_height`, `face_width`,
   `forehead_height`, `nose_bridge_height`, … Verified in sweeps and live
   in Three.js.
2. **`fix_lip_seal.py` windows were hardcoded to realistic anatomy**
   (mouth assumed at z 160–166). On the toon it welded the *nose* (the
   coincident-pair search caught the eyelids) and missed the mouth →
   `cheek_size_lo` / `lip_thickness_lo` / `nose_length_lo` tore the lips
   open. Fixed: all windows now derive from the **teeth mesh bbox**;
   anchor ratios reproduce the old constants exactly on the realistic base
   (regression-verified on a scratch copy — realistic templates untouched).
3. **`chin_size_hi` pushed the lower teeth + mouth-bag through the chin.**
   The toon's giant teeth leave no neutral under-gum skin to measure, so the
   teeth follower inherited chin-tip motion (1.1 cm down+forward vs 0.3 on
   realistic). Fixed with a **closed-bite rule** in
   `add_mouth_follow_morphs.py`: identity morphs never open the bite, so
   lower-teeth deviation from the uppers is capped except for the jaw-shape
   morphs (`jaw_height/width/angle`). Verified clean at `chin_size` 0.9 on
   BOTH toon and realistic.
4. Anchor windows in `add_mouth_follow_morphs.py` also became teeth-scaled
   and median-based (robust to steep morph gradients); realistic follower
   values stayed within ~0.05 cm of the previously verified ones.

## Meta look

v1 keeps the toon geometry (already excellent stylized proportions) and
applies a light material pass (matte skin: roughness 0.72 / spec 0.20 /
normal ≤ 0.5, glossy eyes kept). `stylize_meta_base.py` scaffolds geometry
proportion knobs (head/eye scale) — deliberately off until reviewed on
renders; that plus per-param `exaggeration` tuning is Phase 2's job.
